Movie Sentiment Analysis

Decoding human emotion in cinema through Natural Language Processing. An end-to-end machine learning pipeline transforming 50,000 IMDB reviews into actionable sentiment predictions.

The Data Foundation

The reliability of any Machine Learning model hinges on its dataset. This project utilizes the industry-standard IMDB Dataset, ensuring a robust training ground for binary classification.

Total Reviews Analyzed

50,000

Sentiment Distribution

A perfectly balanced dataset eliminates class bias during training.

Understanding Review Complexity

Movie reviews vary significantly in length. While some users are concise, others provide deep critical analysis. Understanding this distribution helps in setting tokenizer limits.

Distribution of Review Lengths (Word Count)

Most reviews cluster around 150-300 words, creating a right-skewed distribution typical of user-generated content.
